Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

clinch (clinchscenarios.com) is an NFL playoff scenario tool. This page explains what we collect and why.

Without an account

You can use the whole site without signing in. Your picks and saved scenarios are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. We don't receive them.

If you sign in

Sign-in is handled by Google through our database provider, Supabase. We receive your email address, name and profile picture from Google, and a user ID. We use these only to sign you in and to show whose account is active. Scenarios you choose to save to your account (season, team, your picks and do-overs, and the name you give them) are stored in our Supabase database and can be read only by your account.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or send you email. We don't access your Google contacts, Drive or anything beyond basic profile and email.

Share links

Share links contain your picks encoded in the link itself. Anyone with the link can see those picks; the link does not identify you.

Other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts and libraries from public CDNs, which may log standard request data such as IP address. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It doesn't use cookies or track you across sites; it records things like which pages are visited, the referring site and your country. If we add advertising in the future, we will update this page first.

Deleting your data

You can delete saved scenarios from the Saved menu at any time. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email privacy@clinchscenarios.com.
